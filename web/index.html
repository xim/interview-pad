Standing on the shoulders of giants:

Editor and collaboration
Monaco Editor (MIT), with TypeScript and Codicons; monaco-vim (MIT), from CodeMirror's Vim mode; Yjs, y-websocket, y-monaco, y-protocols and lib0 (MIT)
Compilers and runtimes
LLVM: Clang, LLD, libc++, libunwind, clangd (Apache-2.0 with LLVM exception); YoWASP Clang (Apache-2.0, ISC); wasi-libc and musl (MIT, Apache-2.0); browser_wasi_shim (MIT/Apache-2.0); Pyodide (MPL-2.0) with CPython (PSF); Emscripten (MIT); Jedi (MIT); NumPy, pandas, pydantic, pytest and other Python packages
Server
Starlette, Uvicorn and websockets (BSD-3-Clause); pycrdt and pycrdt-websocket (MIT)
Ideas and recipes
wasm-clang by Ben Smith: clang in the browser, and the terminal-style output; clangd-in-browser by Guyutongxue (MIT): the clangd build; clangd's precompiled preamble: the C++ header cache; compiler-rt's UBSan runtime: the wording of UB reports; VS Code's terminal colors: basis of the output palette
